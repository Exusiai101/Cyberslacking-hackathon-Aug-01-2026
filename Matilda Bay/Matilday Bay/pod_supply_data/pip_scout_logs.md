# Pip's Scout Logs — Courier Runs Around Matilda Bay

## Entry 1 — The stockpile is not the story
---------------------------------------------------
Everyone keeps asking me how much water Kestrel has left, or how much medicine sits in
Marrow's stores.

Wrong question.

A big number today means nothing if it is being eaten twice as fast as yesterday. I have started
watching how fast the numbers move, not just what they are.

Note to self: a full stockpile with a rising appetite is a warning. An empty stockpile with nothing
eating it is not.

## Entry 2 — Kestrel's water is fine. Kestrel's food is not.
---------------------------------------------------
Everyone assumes Kestrel is safe because it holds the purification system.

Water was never the problem there. Watch the food store instead — it has been sliding for weeks,
quietly, while everyone worries about the toll booth.

## Entry 3 — Marrow can't drink medicine
---------------------------------------------------
Marrow has more medicine than it will ever need. That was never in doubt.

What nobody tracks is Marrow's own water. Their boats are wrecked, so even fetching enough for
themselves is a struggle now. A pod can be resource-rich and still be in trouble.

## Entry 4 — Tallowfen is not actually in danger
---------------------------------------------------
Tallowfen gets blamed for hoarding, and maybe that's fair. But look at their numbers — they are
not close to running out of anything. Their fear is not matched by their stores.

Do not confuse a pod's reputation for a pod's actual risk. The data does not care what a pod is
afraid of.

## Entry 5 — Reed's End stopped calling for help
---------------------------------------------------
I used to get requests from Reed's End every few days. Then they stopped, right around the second
week.

I don't think things got better there. I think they gave up asking.

If we only respond to who is shouting, we will miss the pod that went quiet.

## Entry 6 — Missing days are not empty days
---------------------------------------------------
Some days there's just... nothing. No report. Especially from the pods furthest out.

Do not read a missing report as a stable one. The distance that makes a pod hard to reach is the
same distance that makes it hard to hear.

## Entry 7 — A delivery is not always the right delivery
---------------------------------------------------
The courier network moves things, but nobody decides what gets carried where. Sometimes food
arrives somewhere that only needed water. It looks like progress. It usually isn't.

## Entry 8 — The drone reads a little low
---------------------------------------------------
Whenever the scout drone files a report instead of an elder, the numbers come in a touch lower
than what an elder would report for the same stockpile. Not wildly off. Just consistently, quietly
low. Worth remembering before assuming a drone report means things got worse overnight.
